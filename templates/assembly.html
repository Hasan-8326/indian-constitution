The Constituent Assembly

The Constituent Assembly was formed in 1946 under the Cabinet Mission Plan. It took 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days to draft the Constitution.

Drafting Committee

On 29 August 1947, the Drafting Committee was appointed to draft a permanent constitution. It was chaired by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar.

Father of the Constitution

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar argued for a wide range of civil liberties, abolition of untouchability, and extensive economic and social rights for women.

Read Biography of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (Wikipedia)

Adoption

The Constitution was adopted on November 26, 1949, signed by 284 members.

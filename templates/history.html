Historical Background

British Constitutional Experiments

The origins of the Indian Constitution lie in the various acts passed during British rule, which slowly introduced representative institutions.

Regulating Act of 1773

Laid the foundation of central administration in India.

Government of India Act 1858

Transferred powers from the East India Company to the British Crown.

Government of India Act 1935

A major source of the Constitution features, introducing provincial autonomy and a federal structure.

The Demand for Sovereignty

The demand for a Constituent Assembly was first made by M.N. Roy in 1934 and officially demanded by the Indian National Congress in 1935.

The Cabinet Mission Plan

In 1946, the British government sent the Cabinet Mission Plan to India to discuss the transfer of power.

The Constitution Today (2025)

The Constitution is a "living document," evolving through amendments and judicial interpretation to meet the needs of a modern India.

Current Debates

  • Digital Rights: Interpretation of Article 21 to include Right to Privacy and Data Protection in the AI era.
  • Federalism: Balancing data and resource sharing between Center and States.
  • Uniform Civil Code: Ongoing discussions regarding Directive Principles (Article 44).

Conclusion

As India navigates the 21st century, the Constitution remains the guiding light, ensuring that the foundational values of Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity persevere.
